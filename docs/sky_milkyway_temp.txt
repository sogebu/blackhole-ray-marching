sky_milkyway_temp.png — the same all-sky Milky Way image, re-encoded as the
light amount and the temperature of one star per pixel (the photo-sky Doppler
option of the viewer; sky_milkyway_temp.json holds its constants)

Original: "Milkyway360-latlon-32kscaled8k-4200k-m14-s4-g1.png"
  by Kevin M. Loch (Wikimedia Commons user Kevinmloch), rendered with
  bsrender (https://github.com/kevinloch/bsrender) from ESA Gaia EDR3 data.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Milkyway360-latlon-32kscaled8k-4200k-m14-s4-g1.png

Changes: alpha channel dropped; every pixel read as one blackbody star
  (light amount a and temperature T) by inverting the renderer's own colour
  model (white at 4200 K, saturation 4); a and a q(T) averaged down from
  8000 x 4000 to 4096 x 2048 (box filter; q = the renderer's colour
  coordinate (B - R) / (R + G + B)); a divided by the mean cos b (b = galactic
  latitude) of the image rows it came from, so that it is a surface
  brightness (a pixel of the equirectangular original holds the light of its
  solid angle, which is proportional to cos b); a and T stored in 12 bits
  each, packed into the RGB channels of a PNG (not a viewable picture).
